What is Rentana?

Rentana is like your rent book, calculator, and property dashboard in one app.

You add your properties, units, and tenants. When rent is paid, Rentana helps you see the money clearly. No guessing. No mixing tenants. No losing track in notebooks or WhatsApp messages.

Very simple

How Rentana works

Even a first-time smartphone user can understand the flow.

1

Add property

Add the building, rooms, houses, rent amount, and tenant details.

2

Tenant pays

The tenant pays rent using the correct account/reference for their unit.

3

You see everything

The app shows paid rent, expected rent, balances, and tenant status.
